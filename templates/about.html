Behind The
Intelligence.

Golden Spy is an information gap detection platform. It examines accessible webpage content using a local keyword-based analysis engine to identify relevant information, missing details and research signals.

CLASSIFIED

Mission Brief

Digital information can be incomplete, inconsistent or outdated. Golden Spy helps researchers examine multiple sources, identify potential information gaps and prepare a verification checklist. Its findings are preliminary signals, not proof of truth.

How It Works

01 / COLLECT

Source Collection

Submit a research question and relevant source URLs. The platform can also attempt automatic source discovery.

02 / ANALYZE

Local Signal Analysis

A keyword-based rule engine examines extracted webpage content, identifies relevant statements and compares topical overlap across sources.

03 / VERIFY

Evidence Review

Review source references, identify unanswered questions and follow the verification checklist using original sources.

Technology Stack

Python Flask HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript BeautifulSoup Requests Vercel
IMPORTANT: Golden Spy currently uses local keyword-based analysis. It does not use an external AI API. Its analysis cannot reliably establish factual truth, detect every contradiction or determine whether information is outdated. Findings must be checked against original sources before relying on them.
