Behind The
Intelligence.

Golden Spy is an AI-assisted information gap detection platform. It examines multiple web sources to identify potential missing details, conflicting claims and outdated signals.

CLASSIFIED

Mission Brief

Digital information can be incomplete, inconsistent or outdated. Golden Spy uses AI-assisted analysis to examine accessible source material, identify potential information gaps and prepare a verification checklist. Its findings are signals for further review, not proof of truth.

How It Works

01 / COLLECT

Source Collection

Submit a research question and relevant source URLs. The platform can also discover sources automatically.

02 / ANALYZE

AI Signal Analysis

Gemini AI analyzes extracted webpage content to identify supported claims, information gaps, possible conflicts and outdated signals.

03 / VERIFY

Evidence Review

Examine source references, review unanswered questions and follow the verification checklist.

Technology Stack

Python Flask Google Gemini AI HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript BeautifulSoup Requests Vercel
IMPORTANT: Golden Spy uses Gemini AI to analyze content extracted from accessible webpages. Source availability and extraction quality may vary. AI-generated findings can contain errors and must be checked against the original sources before relying on them.
